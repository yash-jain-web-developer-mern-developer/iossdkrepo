3. What we do not do

  • No advertising cookies or cross-site tracking; the dashboard stores a session token in the browser tab only (it disappears when you close the tab).
  • No sale of personal data. No behavioural profiles. Device identifiers are not stored.
  • For child-directed apps, COPPA/GDPR-K signals, “limit ad tracking”, GDPR requests without a consent string, and US opt-out strings, device identifiers are ignored entirely and only child-safe, contextual ads are eligible.

In the dashboard (Security tab) you can download all your data and delete your account. 6. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt; optional two-step login; admin accounts require it; encrypted connections to our databases; signed, expiring tracking links; audit logging; regular dependency, container and code scanning. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and authorities of a personal-data breach as the law requires.
