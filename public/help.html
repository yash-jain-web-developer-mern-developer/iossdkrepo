Help center

Practical guides, written to match what the platform actually does today.

Start advertising

  1. Create an advertiser account and verify your email.
  2. Open Wallet and add funds. A campaign's budget is reserved when you submit it.
  3. Open Campaigns → New campaign and follow the four steps: basics, creative, budget, targeting. The preview updates as you type.
  4. New campaigns are screened automatically and usually reviewed by a person before they serve. You will see the status in the campaign list, and can subscribe to a webhook.

Start monetising an app

  1. Create a publisher account, then Apps → Add app. Copy the App ID.
  2. Add the SDK (JavaScript, Kotlin or Swift) and request ads with your App ID. Use test mode first.
  3. Set a floor eCPM, block categories you do not want, and tick child-directed if the app targets children.
  4. Under Earnings and payouts, complete your payout details and identity/tax status so payouts can be approved.
  5. Add the line from Apps → app-ads.txt to your developer website's app-ads.txt file.

Ad formats

Banner, interstitial, native (headline, body, icon, image, sponsor, rating, button), video (https, up to 60 seconds, completion tracking), playable (sandboxed HTML5, always manually reviewed) and rewarded (video with a verified reward). Render playable and partner markup only inside a sandboxed iframe or WebView without same-origin access.

Targeting

Country, platform, category and child-safety, plus: languages, phone or tablet, minimum OS version, Wi-Fi or cellular, hours of the day (UTC), and per-campaign app allow and block lists. A rule you set needs the matching signal in the ad request: a request that does not send the device type never matches a "phones only" campaign.

Creatives and A/B tests

A campaign can carry up to five creatives. MobiAd rotates them with Thompson sampling on click-through rate: better creatives win more traffic, new ones still get tested. See the Reports → Breakdown → Creative view. Adding a creative to a live campaign sends it back through review.

Conversions and CPA

Every accepted click opens your landing URL with mobiad_click_id. When the user converts, your server calls POST /v1/conversion with that id, the event name and an optional value, using a write-scope API key. Last-click attribution applies inside the campaign's window (1 to 30 days). Repeats are never double-counted. With CPA pricing, only the campaign's conversion event is billed, at your bid.

Rewarded video

Request format: "rewarded" and pass your own userId. When playback finishes (and a real impression was seen), MobiAd POSTs a signed callback to your server:

POST https://your-server.example/mobiad-reward
X-MobiAd-Signature: sha256=<HMAC-SHA256(secret, raw body)>
{ "event": "reward", "appId": "app_…", "userId": "player-42", "amount": 25, "item": "coins", "transactionId": "…", "timestamp": 1760000000000 }

Verify the signature, de-duplicate on transactionId, then grant the reward. Never grant it from the client.

The unified auction

MobiAd's own campaigns and approved external demand partners (OpenRTB) compete in one second-price auction. The winner pays just above the best competing bid, never above its own bid, and never below your floor. Partner ads arrive as sandboxed markup (HTML or VAST) and earn you the same revenue share; they are not billed to MobiAd advertisers. Child-directed apps only receive partners approved for children.

Reports

The overview chart shows the last 14 days. Reports has daily performance with CSV download and a breakdown by campaign, creative, app, placement, country or format (depending on your role). Publishers never see advertiser spend.

Billing, invoices and credit notes

Your wallet is calculated from an append-only ledger. After a month closes you can generate its invoice under Wallet → Invoices; invoices are net of tax and tax invoices are not provided. Credit notes issued by support appear below your invoices and add to your wallet.

Payouts

Save your payout details (name, country, method and a payout reference: never full account numbers) and submit identity and tax status for review. Payouts move from requested to approved to paid; the paid step records the external bank or PayPal transaction id. MobiAd does not store identity documents or tax IDs.

Webhooks

Subscribe to events such as campaign.approved, payout.paid or wallet.credited under Developers → Webhooks. Bodies are signed: X-MobiAd-Signature: t=<unix>,v1=<HMAC-SHA256(secret, "<t>.<raw body>")>. Reject old timestamps and de-duplicate on the event id. Failed deliveries retry after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 12 hours.

SDKs and test mode

Pass test: true to receive a canned ad for any format without an auction, billing or tracking: use it to check your rendering. The SDKs enforce good behaviour for you: one impression per ad, no instant clicks, no device ID without consent. The JavaScript SDK can report the impression only once the ad is viewable (50% visible for one second).

Privacy and consent

Pass the user's consent signals with each request. For GDPR, send the TCF v2 consent string: a device identifier is only used when it grants purpose 1, and, if configured, lists MobiAd as a vendor. COPPA, limit-ad-tracking and US-privacy opt-outs disable the identifier entirely. Identifiers are hashed per app and per day and used only for frequency caps.

Troubleshooting

  • No fill (HTTP 204): no approved campaign matches your format, targeting or floor. Try test mode, lower the floor, or check blocked categories.
  • Campaign not serving: it may still be in review, paused, out of budget, or restricted by targeting signals your app does not send.
  • Reward callback not arriving: check the callback URL is public https and answers 2xx; MobiAd retries 5xx responses.
  • Payout blocked: complete payout details and get identity and tax status verified.
